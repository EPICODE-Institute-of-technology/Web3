ERC-20 Permit — sign & submit

This page runs the EIP-2612 permit flow for you. The token owner signs an approval off-chain (no gas, no transaction), and anyone submits that signature on-chain. You do not need to know ethers or any library — just fill the fields and click the buttons in order.

Before you start

  1. Deploy PermitToken.sol from the lab to a network MetaMask can reach (Sepolia, or a local chain). The Remix VM will not work here, because MetaMask cannot sign for its accounts.
  2. Have MetaMask installed, on the same network, with a little test ETH in the account that will submit.

Step 0 — Connect

The connected account is the owner who will sign.

Step 2 — Sign (owner, no gas)

MetaMask shows a signature request, not a transaction. Nothing is sent, nothing is paid.

Step 3 — Submit (anyone)

To show that the owner paid no gas, you may now switch MetaMask to a different account before submitting. That account pays the gas and sends the transaction.
