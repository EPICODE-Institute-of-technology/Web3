Live ERC-20 event listening

A WebSocket Provider lets us subscribe to the Transfer event of the CourseToken in real time. ERC-20 conventions:

  • mint  = Transfer with from = 0x0…0
  • burn  = Transfer with to = 0x0…0
  • transfer = both endpoints non-zero

Open this page side by side with the mini-dApp (or Remix) and watch new events stream in as they are mined.
